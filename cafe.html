Coffee, comfort and conversations

Roast & Rye began with a simple idea: a café should feel like the best seat in your favourite friend's home. We brew every cup fresh from carefully roasted beans, and we prepare our food in-house each morning.

  • Freshly brewed

    Small-batch beans, ground to order.

  • Fresh from the kitchen

    Bakes, sandwiches and breakfasts made daily.

  • Cosy atmosphere

    Soft light, warm wood and room to linger.

  • Friendly service

    A team that remembers your usual order.

Whether you are catching up with friends, studying for exams or taking a laptop break between meetings, there is a table here for you.
